Frequently asked questions

What does the monthly service include?

Content prepared for your firm each month, with your written OK. We agree the content scope before work begins.

Who approves content for my firm?

Your firm reviews the facts, claims and wording and provides written OK before content goes out under its name.

Does Valora contact prospects for us?

The annual option includes up to 30 LinkedIn message drafts per month. Your firm edits and sends them from its own account. Valora does not send these messages on your behalf.

Will this bring us clients?

Valora does not promise clients or other business results. Results vary by firm, market and competition.

How do I learn about the terms?

Send an inquiry about your firm. We'll reply by email with the service scope and terms to review before you decide.

Does this give us consumer inquiries or advisor matches?

No. The service described here is content preparation, not consumer lead delivery. Valora does not currently match consumers with or introduce them to advisors.

Does Valora give financial advice?

Valora publishes educational information. It does not provide investment, tax, legal or personalized financial advice.
