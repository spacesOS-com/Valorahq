QSBS issue spotter

QSBS issue spotter

A rules-based screen that lists the qualified small business stock questions worth reviewing with your CPA. Its only output, on every path, is a list of review items - it never decides eligibility and never computes an exclusion.

Review items for your CPA

—

This screen lists review items only. QSBS qualification is fact-specific across the corporation's history, the stock's issuance and transfers, the holder, and the applicable gain limits - including state-tax treatment, which differs. It cannot be determined by a questionnaire. Review the full picture with your CPA and tax counsel. Rules referenced: IRC Section 1202 as amended; source: thetaxadviser.com Section 1202 coverage. Not tax or legal advice.
