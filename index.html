BEFORE YOU CONTACT AN ADVISOR

Know what to ask.

An advisor's services, approach and fees can differ from another firm's. These questions can help you prepare for a conversation you arrange yourself.

What does the firm help with? What is outside its service? Who will work with you?

How is the firm paid? What fees would you pay, and what other costs might apply?

How does the firm build and review a plan? What decisions remain yours, and what information will it need?

Does the firm work with people facing questions like yours? Ask about relevant experience and the limits of its services.

What does Valora do?

Valora publishes educational guides and calculators. You can also leave your contact details to start an email conversation about a financial topic you're trying to understand.

Does Valora provide personalized financial advice?

No. Our content explains concepts and questions. It does not tell you what to buy, sell or do with your money. Discuss decisions about your circumstances with a qualified professional.

What happens after I submit my details?

We'll email you so you can tell us what you're trying to understand. Valora then reviews your educational request and replies by email. We do not currently select advisors, arrange introductions or book advisor meetings through this process.

How soon will I hear back?

Response timing may vary. Check the email address you provided for a reply about your request.

Is this also for my AI agent?

Yes. The audience includes consumers and the AI agents they use to help them understand financial questions. The same educational scope applies: Valora is not a source of personalized financial advice or advisor matches.
