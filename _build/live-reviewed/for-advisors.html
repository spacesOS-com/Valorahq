What we do

AEO and GEO

We organize and publish your firm's expertise so AI assistants and AI-powered search can read it and cite it where they choose to. We track what AI tools cite and report it to you, including when we cannot tell.

Direct outreach

We build a list of prospects who fit the kind of clients your firm serves, and send LinkedIn outreach to them from your own LinkedIn account.

YouTube

We publish your firm's expertise on Valora's YouTube channel when your firm wants to educate its audience.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your firm. Who you serve and the clients you want.
  2. We write your projection. A written estimate with the assumptions shown so you can check them. It is an estimate, not a result.
  3. We do the work.

Projection

Before work begins we write a projection for your firm, with the assumptions behind it.

  • Search demand: how often people search and ask AI about the topics your firm covers.
  • Projected website visits: our estimate, with the assumptions shown.
  • Projected leads: our estimate, with the assumptions shown.

A projection is an estimate based on stated assumptions. It is not a result, a floor or a guarantee. Valora does not guarantee visitors, leads or clients.

Frequently asked questions

How does Valora help my firm?

Valora does three things: work on how your firm appears in AI answers and search, direct outreach to prospects who fit the clients you serve, and publishing your firm's expertise on Valora's YouTube channel.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) and generative engine optimization (GEO) are the work of making a firm's expertise easy for AI assistants and AI-powered search to read and cite.

Does Valora guarantee new clients?

No. Results depend on your firm, your market and your competition, and visibility builds over time. Valora does not guarantee clients, search rankings, AI citations or video views.

Who sends the outreach?

We send LinkedIn outreach from your own LinkedIn account to prospects on a list we build from the clients your firm serves.

What does it cost?

Plans start at $900 per month, month to month. If you prefer to pay annually, you get 12 months for the price of 10. The price depends on the scope of work for your firm.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The service runs month to month. The annual option is 12 months for the price of 10.

Who owns the YouTube channel?

Valora does. Your firm's expertise is published on Valora's YouTube channel.

Is Valora an investment adviser?

No. Valora is not a registered investment adviser and does not provide investment advice. Valora provides marketing services to advisory firms.
