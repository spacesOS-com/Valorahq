A first meeting, not a first pitch

01 — The conversation

A good advisor asks
about your life before
your portfolio.

Retirement at sixty, or a business you never sell. A second home, a first grandchild, a sabbatical that lasts a year. A good plan writes down the actual outcomes before touching a single allocation.

Concentration in one stock. A tax bill you did not see coming. Aging parents and a plan that never accounted for them. The risks that matter are rarely the ones in the brochure.

Use the first conversation to understand the advisor's approach and whether it fits your needs.

Common questions

Is Valora free to use?

Yes. Exploring advisors on Valora is free, with no obligation, and we never sell your information.

Does Valora provide investment advice?

No. Valora is an independent platform that provides financial education and helps consumers discover financial advisors. Valora does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice — financial advisory services are provided independently by the advisors you choose to contact.

How does Valora match me with an advisor?

You answer a few questions about your situation — what you're solving for, your assets, and your circumstances. Valora surfaces advisors who may fit, and you review their profiles and decide who, if anyone, to contact.

Who are the advisors on Valora?

Independent, fiduciary financial advisors — not Valora employees. Valora is an independent platform that helps you discover them; it isn't an advisory firm itself.

How soon will I hear back after I submit the form?

After you submit, we review your request and email you about next steps. Timing may vary.
