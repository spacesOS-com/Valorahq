Frequently Asked Questions

What is Spaces building for advisors?

Spaces is developing a program that combines LinkedIn outreach and work on AI-search visibility. Delivery and the detailed written terms are still being validated. Ask what is ready now and what remains in development.

Are introductions exclusive or pre-qualified today?

Do not assume that. Spaces is not claiming an established exclusive lead-delivery stream. The proposed commitment uses a client profile agreed in writing and counts unique, profile-matched introductions across both channels; the final agreement will define the evidence and process.

What should I expect from a readiness review?

The form requests a review. A completed review can show examples of how your firm appears in AI answers at the time of the check. It does not guarantee a search placement, introduction, meeting, or client.

What are the proposed founding advisor terms?

The proposed first-ten rate is $1,500 per month, billed quarterly on a 12-month commitment. The proposed delivery commitment is 15 profile-matched introductions in six months across LinkedIn and AI-search combined. The final agreement will define qualification, onboarding, and billing-pause treatment; ask us for written terms before signing.

Who provides financial advice to clients?

The independent advisor, under that firm’s own agreement and applicable disclosures. Spaces is developing an acquisition program and does not provide the client’s investment, tax, or legal advice.
